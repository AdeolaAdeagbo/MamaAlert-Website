How to Delete Your MamaAlert Account

Quick steps to permanently remove your account and data

Last updated: March 2026

Contents

  1. Delete in the app
  2. Delete by email
  3. What gets deleted
  4. What may be retained

1. Delete in the app

Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account > Confirm deletion. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once confirmed.

2. Delete by email

Send an email to mymamalert@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request and include your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your profile and personal information
  • All health logs, symptoms and appointment records
  • Emergency contact information
  • Chat history with Nura
  • Community posts and interactions

4. What may be retained

  • Anonymised, non-identifiable usage data for research
  • Transaction records required by law

Need help?

If you run into issues deleting your account, contact our support team.

mymamalert@gmail.com

We aim to respond to all deletion requests within 30 days.

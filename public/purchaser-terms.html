5. How to Cancel or Manage Your Subscription

Subscriptions purchased on Android are billed through Google Play. To cancel or manage your Subscription, open the Google Play Store on your device, go to your account, select your subscription, and select "Cancel subscription" or "Manage subscription".

Subscriptions purchased on iOS are billed through the Apple App Store. To cancel or manage your Subscription, open the Settings app on your device, select your Apple ID, select Subscriptions, select your MamaAlert subscription, and select "Cancel Subscription". You can also manage the subscription from your order history in the App Store.

Purchases made on any other platform must be managed through that platform. Cancelling with us does not cancel a subscription bought elsewhere.

Removing the app from your device does not cancel your Subscription and will not stop billing.

6. Refunds

Refunds for purchases made through Google Play are handled by Google in line with the Google Play refund policy. To request a refund, contact us first and then follow Google Play's refund process; where Google approves a refund, the amount is returned to your original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days depending on your payment provider.

Refunds for purchases made through the Apple App Store are handled by Apple in line with the Apple refund policy. Where a refund is approved, Apple returns the amount to your original payment method.

For purchases made directly with MamaAlert (outside Google Play and the Apple App Store), we offer a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not substantially used the paid features. After 7 days, we refund the unused portion of the current billing period on a pro-rata basis.

To request a refund, email us at the support address in the "Contact" section below with your account email, the date of purchase and the reason for the request. Refunds are not issued for renewals you did not intend where a cancellation could have been made before renewal, or where features were used extensively.

Once a refund is issued, your Premium access ends immediately.
